System Architecture & Technical Specifications

Deep Neural Network regression pipeline with domain adjustments

Neural Network Topology

  • Architecture: Deep Sequential Multi-Layer Perceptron (TensorFlow 2.x & Keras)
  • Input Dimension: 6 core mechanical features (Cylinders, Horsepower, Weight, Acceleration, Year, Origin)
  • Regularization: Batch Normalization and 30% Dropout layers to eliminate overfitting
  • Loss & Metric: Mean Absolute Error (MAE) and Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE)

Fuel & Blend Chemistry Multipliers

  • Diesel Engine: Higher compression ratio & energy density (+20% thermal efficiency)
  • E0 Pure Petrol: 100% pure gasoline energy density benchmark (+2.5% vs E10)
  • E10 Blend (Standard): 10% ethanol blend (baseline calibration)
  • E20 Blend (Modern): 20% ethanol blend (~4.5% energy density reduction compensated by high octane)
